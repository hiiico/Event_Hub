About EventHub

EventHub was created to help people discover and share local events. We believe that community gatherings enrich lives and strengthen neighbourhoods.

Our platform empowers anyone to create an event – from a small book club meeting to a large music festival – and connect with others who share their interests.

Built with ❤️ by a team of developers passionate about community building. EventHub is open source and free to use.

Our Mission

To make event discovery simple, inclusive, and fun. We aim to reduce the friction of organising and attending events, so you can focus on what matters – the experience.
